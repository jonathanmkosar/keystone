# Turn inspector

Folder: `internal/inspector`, joined to a turn and served by `internal/turn/inspect.go` and `internal/turn/inspectroutes.go`. The screen is in `web/app/src/Inspector.tsx`.

Shows what went into each turn, by section, with tokens and cost, and lets a person pin a message or take a recalled item or a pinned message out of the chat's next turn. Written in full with the rest of P5-04.
