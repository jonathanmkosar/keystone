## What was done

- A new package, `internal/inspector`. It reads no setting and opens no connection.
  - `Calls` joins a turn's context records, kept by step, to its usage records, which carry the turn's request id and no step: a call's run of records ends at the one that is `ok` or `stopped`, or at the kind's last. It returns each call with its records and their total, and the totals of every record of the turn (decision 0270).
  - Skips, over a new table `chat_skips` (migration `20261125T152500_chat_skips.sql`): `Add`, `Remove`, `Waiting`, `Claim`, `OfTurn`, `Check`. The turn that builds the next first model call claims a chat's waiting skips, and every call of that turn leaves them out; no other turn does (decision 0271).
  - `LeaveOut` marks the recalled items and pinned messages the skips name as removed.
- The context builder (`internal/contextbuild`) gained `Ref` and `Removed` on `Item` and `ItemRecord`, `Removed` and `Ref` on `PartRecord`, and `SummaryRef` on its input. A removed item is not sent and holds no room, even pinned, and is recorded as removed with the tokens it would have taken (decision 0272).
- In `internal/turn`, two new files: `inspect.go` (`leaveOut` for `build`, the names for items, `InspectReply`, `SkipsReply`, the handlers) and `inspectroutes.go` (`InspectorRoutes`: `GET /chats/{id}/turns/{turn}/inspect`, `GET /chats/{id}/skips`, `PUT` and `DELETE /chats/{id}/skips/{kind}/{ref}`). A few changed lines elsewhere, listed under "For the integrator".
- The screen, in new files: `Inspector.tsx` and `inspector.css` (the panel), `MessageActions.tsx` (Pin and Inspect beside Copy), `pins.ts` (a chat's pins). Lines in `api.ts`, `Icon.tsx`, `layout.ts`, `Conversation.tsx` and `Chat.tsx`. `web/dist/` rebuilt.

## What was learned

- The usage records were built for the month and the cap, not for one turn, and carry no step. Adding one would have meant changing the models lane's append-only table and the runner's call path, which other lanes are working beside. Order is enough: the runner writes a failed model's record before trying the next, and a chat call that does not end `ok` or `stopped` ends the turn. The totals do not depend on the grouping at all, which is what the proof needs.
- "For the next turn" had to be pinned down. Claiming at the first call of a turn, and reading the same skips in its later calls, is the one answer that keeps an item out of a whole turn of tool steps and gives it back afterwards, and it sends a skip made while a turn waits in the queue to that turn.
- Marking a removed item on the builder's input, not filtering it out, keeps the record the request, keeps the builder's rebuild test meaningful, and lets the next turn's inspector say "left out by you".
- Taking a sensitive page out of a call could have been allowed to unhold the route. It is not: the route and the chat's mark are decided from what recall found, so both of fixed rule 3's guards are exactly as they were.
- A pinned message in a turn that is sent leaves the `pinned` part empty. The panel's first draft hid it as "nothing in"; it now shows a part with items, as "in their turns".
- The new tests passed on their first run, so each was broken on purpose: the turn's call to `leaveOut` taken out (both removal proofs failed, with the page and the pin in the audit log), every usage record made its own call (the totals test failed on the fallback), and the builder made to keep removed items (both builder tests failed). Each fault was taken out again.
