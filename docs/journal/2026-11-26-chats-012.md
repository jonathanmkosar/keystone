## What was done

- **The contract first**, in the item's plan: `GET /chats/table` and `POST /chats/delete`, both in a new file, `internal/turn/manage.go`, listed in `internal/app` by one added line. No migration.
- **The search by chat** (`internal/chat/bychat.go`, decision 0550). `chat.Search` returns at most fifty hits, several of them in one chat, so it cannot count the chats that match, page them or be joined with a filter. `SearchByChat` reads the same two full-text tables with the same reading of a query and returns every chat once, title matches first; `Excerpts` cuts the title and the best message's snippet into parts with the matching words marked, for the rows of one page. `Search` and its route are untouched.
- **The table** (decision 0551). One query reads every chat with a count of its messages and its marks, from the tables of the seven parts that keep them: project, a task's chat, a sub-agent's, open share links, the import's mark, the hold for sensitive content, incognito, and whether a turn, a task or a sub-agent is at work. Filters (starred, imported, in a project, task, sub-agent), the order (last activity, title, size, best match) and the page are worked out in Go; only the page's rows are given their summary, the import's details and the excerpt. `ids=true` gives the ids of every chat that matches, for "select all that match".
- **Deleting many** (decisions 0552, 0553, 0554). Each chat goes by `chat.Delete`, in its own transaction, with its queue in the runner held so no turn can start in between. A chat with a turn queued or running, or with a task or a sub-agent that has not ended in it or for it, is left and named with its reason; nothing is stopped. Another person's id is `not_found`, as any id that is not the caller's is. Each chat is an entry in the audit log under the call's request id, with the action its own delete would have. More ids than `chats.delete_batch_max` is refused whole.
- **The screen** (`web/app/src/ManageChats.tsx`, `manageCalls.ts`, `manage-chats.css`): the search box, five filter chips, the table with its marks and a line of snippet or summary, loading as the list's end comes into view, select all shown, "Select all N that match", the bar with the count and Delete, the confirmation, and a note afterwards that names each chat that was left. `pages.tsx` gives `Chats` from the new file; `App.tsx` is untouched.
- **On a phone**: rows of a few short lines, nothing wider than the screen, a 44 pixel square for each checkbox, sorting by a native select, no box that takes the focus by itself, 16px text boxes, and the confirmation placed by `window.visualViewport`.
- **Three settings**, a person's own: `chats.table_page_rows` (50), `chats.table_select_max` (2000), `chats.delete_batch_max` (100).
- **The proof**: one test in `internal/chat`, three in `internal/turn`, the isolation drill's walk with the first person's chat ids in the batch and their words in the table's search, and the new standing check `manage chats` on the real server.
- The write-up: `docs/architecture/manage-chats.md`, decisions 0550 to 0554, a part row and five decision rows in the overview, a paragraph in `tools.md`, and a section each in `web.md`, `turns.md` and `chat.md`. `docs/api/openapi.json` regenerated.

## What was learned

- The small words for a screen reader (`.offscreen`, absolutely positioned with no positioned parent) are placed against the page, not against the list they scroll in. One in each of 140 rows made the page itself 2,000 pixels tall inside a frame that is meant never to scroll, and opening the confirmation then slid the whole frame up. A row's checkbox now carries its name as `aria-label`, and the page is exactly as tall as the screen.
- A `<label>` given `role="cell"` stops being a label: its checkbox read as "on". The cell is now a span around the label.
- Cookies do not know ports. This session's first delete in the browser answered 401, a minute after signing in on `127.0.0.1:7960`: most likely another lane signed in to its own server on `127.0.0.1`, on another port, in the same browser, and its cookie took this one's place (it was not proven, only that the session's cookie was no longer this server's). The server was then opened as `p406.localhost:7960`, a name of its own, and the session held to the end. A lane that looks at a screen while others do should use a name of its own.
- A title cut to one line on a phone hides the very word a search found in it. On a phone a title now takes two lines.
- An observer of the list's end does not fire while the browser pane draws nothing, so a script that scrolled and counted rows saw no more of them until a screenshot was taken. The rows had loaded; the measurement was early.
- A minute by the clock is many tool calls. A `verify` three minutes into its run was taken for hung and stopped; its output had been piped through `tail`, which prints nothing until the end. It was run again with its output in a file, and the clock was read before judging anything slow.
- Holding a chat's queue is one map entry in the runner, the one the goroutine that works a chat's turns takes. Taking it for the length of a delete closes the gap between "nothing is running" and "deleted", with no new lock.
