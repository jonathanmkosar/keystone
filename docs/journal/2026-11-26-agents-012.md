# Agents 012, 2026-11-26

Item: P12-04, Google from New Keystone, asked for by the owner. Built by the integrator in the main checkout, not in a lane.

## What was done

- `connectors.AdoptGoogle` (`internal/connectors/googleadopt.go`) takes over a Google sign-in made elsewhere on this machine with the same client: the refresh token into the vault as `connector.google`, the account and scopes into `google_accounts`, and the connector of each service the scopes cover added or switched on. `ensureGoogle` is the handler's `ensure` moved out so both use it; the handler's method is now one line.
- `tools/tenant connector google <tenant-id> <account address> <scope> ...` reads the token from standard input and prints no part of it.
- Two tests: a Calendar sign-in becomes the person's sign-in with Calendar on and no Gmail, twice over without doubling, and the other person has none of it; what does not read as a sign-in is refused and nothing is kept.
- `docs/architecture/google.md` and `docs/runbooks/google.md` each gained a section.

## Why it works

New Keystone asked for `openid email` and `calendar.events.owned`, the same Calendar scope as Keystone's, with offline access, and the owner's vault already holds that client's id and secret. A refresh token is bound to its client and scopes, not to a redirect address, so it serves here unchanged.

## Not done

- Gmail. New Keystone never asked for it. It needs the Gmail API on, its two scopes and Keystone's redirect address on the client, then Connect Google on Settings → Connectors.
- No model has called a Calendar tool with the brought sign-in; the live check is `connector check`.

## For the owner

- Disconnecting Google in either program revokes the sign-in at Google for both.
