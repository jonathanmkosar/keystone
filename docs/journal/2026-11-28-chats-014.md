## What was done

- **Two settings** in `settings/models.json`, after `router.auto_default`: `router.may_switch_off` (bool, true, tenant-admin) and `router.allowed_models` (string, empty, tenant-admin). With P14-03's three and P14-04's one, that is the spec's six. The admin sets them with `go run ./tools/tenant setting set <tenant-id> <key> <value>`, which already stores a `tenant-admin` setting as the admin, until P15-03's admin settings screen draws them from the registry; a new test proves it (`tools/tenant/setting_policy_test.go`).
- **The policy** (`internal/turn/policy.go`, new): `Policy` (`Allowed`, `MaySwitchOff`, `AutoOffered`), `ParseAllowed`, `Allows`, `AutoRequired`, `Refusal`, and `Runner.PolicyFor` for other lanes. Auto is required only while it is offered, that is while the ladder has a usable step (decision 0720).
- **The refusals**: 403 `auto_required` for a message naming a model while Auto is required, checked first; 403 `model_not_allowed` for a model the person may not pick by hand, with its label. Checked by `POST /chats/{id}/turns` before anything is queued, and again in `openTurn` when the turn leaves the queue, for every turn, `Runner.Send` included; a turn that has started, and a stronger model, are not checked again (decisions 0721, 0724).
- **Chats on Auto**: `POST /chats` stores a new chat on Auto while Auto is required, whatever `router.auto_default` says; any chat off Auto is offered as on Auto and put on it by its next turn naming no model (decision 0722).
- **The model choice** (`withPolicy`, which the three returns of `choice` now go through): `allowed` is the allowed subset of `models`, `may_switch_off` the setting (true with no ladder), `auto` true while Auto is required, and `model` chosen among the allowed only, so `chats.default_model` and the chat's last model outside the list are passed over (decision 0723).
- **The screens** (`web/app/src/policyWords.ts` and its test, new; small edits to `ModelPicker.tsx`, `modelPicks.ts`, `turns.ts`, `Chat.tsx`, `api.ts`, `package.json`): a model the person may not pick is greyed with the reason; while Auto is required the model rows are hidden and one line says why; the new chat screen reads both settings; a pick the server would refuse is let go when the choice is read; a refusal shows the server's sentence; the composer no longer says there is no model in a chat on Auto (decision 0725).
- The contract's comments: `ModelChoice.may_switch_off` and `allowed`, `SendBody.model`, and the summaries of `POST /chats` and `POST /chats/{id}/turns`; `docs/api/openapi.json` regenerated.
- The write-up: `docs/architecture/router-policy.md` (new) with its `componentDocs` lines (`tools/verify/routerpolicy.go`, new), a part row and six decision rows in the overview, short additions to `router.md`, `turns.md` and `web.md`, decisions 0720 to 0725.

## What was learned

- The spec's refusal "on chat create" has nothing to refuse: `POST /chats` takes no body. What a create can do is start the chat on Auto, and the turn makes the rest hold for chats made any other way.
- "May not leave Auto" with an empty ladder would refuse every message a person sends. Tying it to the ladder the picker shows keeps the screen and the server on one rule.
- A chat on Auto with no model allowed by hand showed "There is no model to answer in this chat" under the composer, a warning P14-04 never met because every person then had a model. The screen in the browser found it, not the tests.
- The test tenant's one model answers its turns whatever is picked, so its picker can show "Pick a model" off Auto when the list leaves it out. Left as it is: an ordinary person in the same state gets `no_model` and the choice's reason.
- The browser package's idle test waited for the fake browser to close and then read the pool, which closes a moment later; under seven lanes' load it failed in two verify runs. The loop now waits for both, the condition the test checks, with the same deadline.
