# Core 012, 2026-11-26

Item provisional: P11-03, Operations and Monitor. Built in its own worktree, on the branch `core/p11-03-operations`, cut from `reboot` at df91864, on the web shell (P4-01), whose only open part is the mockups. The doctor did not list the item as ready because P4-01 is provisional; the integrator decided to build it now.

The owner runs background tasks, schedules and three models every day, and until now there was no one place to see what was running, which model was failing, or how close the install was to a limit. This pass made that place: an Operations page and a notice on the home screen, read from the parts that keep the facts, summed no second way, with a trend for every limit from snapshots the server keeps.

## What was done

- A new package, `internal/operations`. `Read(ctx, store, reg, src, now)` is the one function behind `GET /api/v1/operations`, in five parts:
  - **Jobs**: tasks and scheduled runs by state (with paused and waiting), the newest `operations.jobs_shown`, the stuck (open, not paused or waiting, a round older than `operations.stuck_after`) and the failed within the health window. Through `jobs.List` and `jobs.Tally`, new in `internal/jobs/list.go`, which read rows through the package's own `scanJob`. A task's spend is summed from its usage records.
  - **Schedules**, through the scheduler's own `List`, handed to the route.
  - **Model health** over `operations.health_window` from the usage records: calls, ok, errors, refusals, stopped, the median time of the calls sent, cost, a state (good, errors at `operations.model_error_watch_percent`, failing, refused, idle) and the last error's words from the audit log, through `audit.Scrub`.
  - **Recent traces**: the newest request ids among the person's turns, a chat turn or every round of a run, with calls, errors, refusals, tool steps, tokens and cost from its usage records, its outcome, and the chat and turn the inspector opens on.
  - **Limits**: the monthly cap and the memory reserve from `usagereport.Read` with the month; the disk, the volume that holds the data folder, as an install fact; the person's database, files and artifacts against rooms the admin sets; and the budget of each run under way. Each has its share, a watch level (the cap's is `usage.cap_warn_percent`, the rest are settings), a state and a trend.
- `Watch` and `GET /api/v1/operations/watch`: the limits at or past their watch level, read by the same `Limits`, for the home screen.
- Trends: migration `20261126T001900_operations.sql` adds `operations_snapshots` to each tenant's database. A sampler under the turn runner, started by `internal/app` after the scheduler, records every tenant's limits at once and every `operations.snapshot_every` (an hour), skipping a tenant sampled within the interval, and removes snapshots past `operations.snapshot_keep`. A trend is a least-squares line through the snapshots in `operations.trend_window` and the reading now: growth a day, when it fills, and for the cap and the reserve what it reaches by the first of next month. A run's points are its usage records.
- Seventeen settings in `settings/core.json`, five for the install and twelve for each person.
- The screens, provisional: the Operations page at `/operations` (sidebar after Memory, and a Settings tab that goes to it) with tiles, needs attention, room to grow, models, tasks, schedules and traces, and the notice "Running out of room" on the home screen. Bars and trend lines are inline SVG from the tokens. New files `Operations.tsx`, `OperationsNotice.tsx`, `operationsCalls.ts`, `operations.css`.
- The standing check `operations`, in `tools/verify/operations.go`, makes `verify` 31 of 31.
- Docs: `docs/architecture/operations.md`, decisions 0460 to 0463, rows in the overview and `internal/README.md`, paragraphs in `tools.md` and `web.md`, and lines in `jobs.md`, `schedules.md`, `unattended-spend.md`, `usage-report.md`, `usage.md`, `audit.md` and `settings-screens.md`. The OpenAPI document is regenerated.

## What was learned

- The data folder's total cannot be shown to one person of two: less their own folder, it is the other person's size. The disk is the volume instead, which counts everything on it, and each person sees their own folder as three limits (decision 0460).
- A task's row keeps a spend written from its turns, and the budget's gate sums the usage records. In the browser check the two disagreed on invented rows, so a task's line now reads the records, as the gate does (decision 0462). The point is that Operations must show what the gate will do.
- `audit.List` returns whole entries; a model call's entry holds its whole request. Twenty traces through it would read megabytes to show a tool's name, so the traces read a few columns with their own queries.
- An absolutely placed, hidden table heading escaped its scrolling card and made the whole page scroll sideways at phone width. The card is now `position: relative`.
- The registry refuses a model endpoint with a user name in it and a local model on a public host, so the health tests' invented endpoints had to be a plain host and a `.home.arpa` one. The guard works.
- A month's first day leaves the cap's trend almost no history; the check counts the snapshots each limit's window should hold instead of a fixed number, so it does not fail on the first of a month.
