# Interface 005, 2026-11-25

Item provisional: P9-02, uploads. The server side is complete; the screens wait on the workspace mockups (P1-06).

Built in its own worktree, on the branch `interface/p9-02-uploads`, cut from `reboot` at 798e627, and pushed after every commit.

## What was done

- **The store**, a new package `internal/uploads`. A file is written to the tenant's `files/` folder under its SHA-256 through `Store.Files()`, tied to its chat in `chat_files`, with a row in `uploads` (migration `20261125T213700_uploads.sql`). An image or a PDF is known by its bytes, a text file by its ending and by being UTF-8; anything else is refused with the reason. An upload a message holds cannot be removed; it goes with the chat (decision 0380).
- **Text extraction**: plain text, Markdown, code, CSV and JSON as they are, and HTML to its words with a small reader on the standard library. No PDF reader is a dependency, so a PDF is sent whole to a model that takes files and its text is not read: provisional (decision 0381).
- **Six routes**: add (the body is the file, `?name=`), list (with the notices), one (with the text read), its bytes (an image inline; anything else as a download, under a sandboxing policy), remove, and `GET /uploads/limits`. The add route takes a body as large as `uploads.max_bytes` through a new field, `api.Route.MaxBodyBytes` (decision 0384). `docs/api/openapi.json` regenerated.
- **Into a call**: `uploads.Prepare`, called for every model call from `internal/turn/uploads.go`, readies the branch for the least of the route: an image as its bytes only when every model the call can reach can see, a note in its place otherwise; a text file's text into `contextbuild.Input.Attached`, newest first, cut to a setting; a document whole only to a route that takes files. What each call was given and withheld is kept in `upload_calls` and returned by the inspector as `uploads`.
- **Routing by capability**: a turn whose message holds an image, or whose branch does when the chat's model can see, asks the route for vision through `routing.Ask.Needs`, so it goes to the first fallback that can when the chat's model cannot; with none, the image is left out and the person told. The chat keeps the person's model (decision 0382).
- **The adapters**: each of the three refuses an image for an entry without vision before anything is sent (decision 0383), proven in a `vision_test.go` each against a stand-in.
- **The screen**, provisional: a plus button and dropping files on the composer, chips with remove, upload at send, thumbnails and downloads in the person's message, notices above a reply, the attachments part's items and an Images and files section in the inspector. New files `Attachments.tsx`, `uploads.ts`, `uploads.css`.
- **The proof**: tests in `internal/uploads`, `internal/turn/uploads_test.go`, the adapters and `internal/api`, and a new `verify` check, `uploads`. `verify` is 24 of 24.
- **Settings**: a new `settings/uploads.json`, lane `uploads`, ten settings.
- The write-up: `docs/architecture/uploads.md` with its `componentDocs` line (an `init` in `tools/verify/uploads.go`) and overview row, decisions 0380 to 0384, and a paragraph each in `context.md`, `inspector.md`, `routing.md`, `api.md`, `chat.md`, `seam.md`, `turns.md`, `web.md`, `tools.md` and the three adapter docs.

## What was learned

- **Most of the seam was ready.** The image block with its `ref`, `routing.Ask.Needs` with its capability filter, `chat_files` with deletion by chat, the `Attached` slot and the attachments share were all there. What was missing was the store, the decision of what a call gets, and the turn asking for vision.
- **A call is built once for its whole route**, so the image rule has the same shape as the sensitive-content rule: built for the least of the route. Asking the route for vision keeps that from blinding a model that can see: when the turn has an image, models without vision are not in the route at all.
- **The chat's model moved by accident.** The turn writes its route's first model on its row, and the next turn takes it as the chat's. A turn moved to a fallback for an image would have moved the chat for good, so the turn writes the person's model when that was the only reason.
- **The API's 1 MiB body limit is applied before routing**, so no handler could take a photo. A route-level limit, applied in the same place, was the smallest change.
- **The inspector screen keeps only the fields it knows**: `inspectTurn` builds its own object, so the new `uploads` field was dropped until `api.ts` passed it on. Found in the browser.
- **The browser pane was hidden**, so its viewport was 0 wide and the composer measured itself as a tall column. Setting a viewport on the tab gave a true layout; it was not a fault of the page.
- Tests that passed at once were broken on purpose: the adapter's check, the least-of-route rule and the route's vision request each made their tests fail when taken out, and the `uploads` check reported four problems with the vision gate in `Prepare` taken out.
