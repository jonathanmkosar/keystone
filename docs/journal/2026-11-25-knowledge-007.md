## What was learned

- Waymarks has no call that removes a source. Its sources are kept unchanged, erasing is the owner's alone with the owner's token, and issues point at pages, under a grant none of Keystone's tokens has. So "forget deletes what Keystone's intake added" cannot be done in Waymarks; withdrawing in Keystone is what can (decision 0371).
- Two tests reached into the adapter's own type through `knowledge.Open`, so wrapping the store in `Open` broke them. `Unwrap` fixed both, and wrapping in one place means no later caller can forget the withdrawn list.
- The memory test's helper made turn ids from the clock and two came out the same on Windows once the package had more tests. It now numbers them.
- On a case-insensitive disk `Memory.tsx` and `memory.ts` are one name to TypeScript; the calls became `memoryCalls.ts`.
- `gofmt` aligns trailing comments in a run of lines, so two new lines with comments beside `MemoryRoutes` would have changed the lines above them. A comment line of their own keeps the old lines as they were.
- The check passed first time, which proves nothing about the check. Three faults were put in by hand: the incognito chat searched, the source withdrawn instead of removed, and the recap made. The first and last were caught as written. The second was caught only by the `removed` flag, because the check read the source through `Open`, which hides a withdrawn one; it now reads through the built-in store's own handle, and the faults were taken out again.

## Questions for the owner

1. With Waymarks, a forgotten source is withdrawn in Keystone and stays in Waymarks until you erase it there. Should Keystone also tell Waymarks' agents, so the upkeep jobs do not compile it into a page?

   Recommended: yes, once the upkeep jobs exist (P8-06): a short notice source under the intake token that names the withdrawn source's number and asks for it to be erased, without its words. An issue needs a page and the raise-issues grant, which none of Keystone's tokens has.

   Assumed meanwhile: nothing is sent to Waymarks but the source holding the facts that are kept (decision 0371).

   Provisional because of it: nothing.

2. An incognito chat's calls stay in the audit log, what was sent and what came back, because every call is recorded (fixed rule 4). Is that what you want of incognito?

   Recommended: yes, as built. The rule cannot be switched off, the log is read by no model and no search, and it is how a call can be accounted for. The chat record, memory, recaps and past-chat search keep nothing.

   Assumed meanwhile: as recommended.

   Provisional because of it: nothing.

3. How long is an incognito chat that was not left kept: is a day right?

   Recommended: 24 hours from its last message, `knowledge.incognito_keep`, which each person can change (5m to 720h). Leaving it deletes it at once.

   Assumed meanwhile: 24 hours.

   Provisional because of it: nothing.
