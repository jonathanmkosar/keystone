## What was learned

- **The best row was in the sketch that lost.** The compact list was the calmest by far, and it lost because the short line was not in it and a switch took three taps. Its row, with the short line under it and opened in place, is what was built. Sketching all three on the real registry is what showed it: none of that was visible in the plan.
- **A value as plain text is what makes a list of settings calm.** A boxed number in every row took a third of a phone's width and made each row a form.
- **"Changed" has to mean "differs from the default"**, not "has a stored value". The theme switch stores "dark" so the choice follows a person to another browser, and a row that says "Theme, Changed, dark" beside a default of dark is wrong to the eye. `isChanged` is the one place that says so.
- **A button's target can be the whole row without the row being the button**: the name is the button and a pseudo-element stretches its target, so a screen reader hears a name and a description and the switch stays a button of its own.
- **`.input` is 15px**, so every box on every settings screen zoomed the page on a phone. One rule under `.settings` at phone width makes them 16px.
- The tests of `settingsView.ts` passed at once, so three were broken on purpose: a row carrying the description failed one, a group dropped failed two, and an opened row carrying the short line failed one.
- A shell command that chains `git` with other commands is refused in an isolated worktree; plain separate commands are not.
