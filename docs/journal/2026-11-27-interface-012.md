# Interface 012, 2026-11-27

P10-06, Location. The owner asked for "GPS" from his iPhone after a chat in which the model asked which city he was in to look up the weather, and chose this design: per message. A "Use my location" control behind the composer's `+` asks the phone for its position, and the position, rounded to about a kilometre, goes with that message only.

Built in its own worktree, on the branch `interface/p10-06-location`, cut from `reboot` at a0def61.

## What was done

- **The message** (decision 0600): `location: {"latitude", "longitude"}` on the send body, beside `text` or `blocks`. `Runner.checkLocation` rounds it again to `location.decimals` and refuses it, with 400 and before a turn is made, while `location.share` is off, on a retry, past ±90 or ±180, or with a field a location does not have. `keepLocation` writes it beside the person's message in a new table, `message_locations`, with its decimals and the line, so it goes with the message and the chat. `location` is on the message in `GET /chats/{id}` and the `message` event, and on the inspector's reply.
- **The model's request** (decision 0601): one text block before the person's words in that message, by default "The person's approximate location: 12.35, -45.68 (rounded to about 1 km), shared with this message." (`location.line`). Written once, when the message is placed, and added in `buildMessages`, so every later call (tool steps, later turns, compaction's prefix check, the inspector's previews) sends the same bytes. The standing start is untouched. No reverse geocoding.
- **The header** (decision 0602): `h.Add("Permissions-Policy", "geolocation=(self)")` after the microphone's `h.Set` line; a browser joins the two into `microphone=(self), geolocation=(self)`. Nothing else in the headers changed.
- **The control** (decision 0603): `location.ts` (no imports: the settings, `askPosition`, the rounding, every word) and `LocationControl.tsx`: a map-pin button just after the attach button on a desktop; on a phone a row of the `+` sheet with its state and the hint on a line under it; the chip "Location: about 1 km" over the box with a remove button; "Sent with location" under the person's bubble; and a Location section in the inspector. The position is asked for only in the tap, once, at low accuracy and not cached, and the composer's one send takes it (`location.take()`), so the next message has none.
- **Settings**, a new `settings/location.json`, lane `location`: `location.share` (true), `location.decimals` (2, one of 1 to 4), `location.timeout` (10s), `location.line`.
- **The proof**: `internal/turn/location_test.go` (five tests), a header test in `cmd/keystone/serve_test.go`, `web/app/test/location.test.ts` (eight tests), and a standing check, `location`, in `tools/verify/location.go`.
- The write-up: a section in `web.md`, "Location per message", a paragraph in `turns.md`, one in `tools.md`, decisions 0600 to 0603 with their rows in `overview.md`, the item's Plan, Result and status.

## What was learned

- **The fake provider still answers a located message**: it matches a script on the whole text or on the last text block, so the line as a block of its own leaves the person's words where a recorded turn finds them. That made the browser check possible with the test tenant's recorded replies.
- **A field beats a block kind**: a new block would have reached the seam's validation, three adapters, uploads, the search, the recap, the memory pass, the share page and the import. The memory pass reads the record, not the request, so it never sees the position.
- **The microphone's header line is pinned by the dictation check**, which this item may not change; a second `Permissions-Policy` line is the same policy to a browser and leaves that line, its test and its check as they were.
- **The inspector's call keeps only the fields it knows** (`inspectTurn` in `api.ts`), so a new field on the reply has to be added there too; the browser check found it.
- **A source check has to look past a type**: the first version of the `location` check found `tap: () =>` in the control's type before its code, and failed on correct code. It now looks for the function bodies.
