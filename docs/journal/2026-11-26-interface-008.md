## What was done

- **The machine**, a new `web/app/src/dictation.ts` with no imports: `createDictation` is given a recognizer factory, a clock, the settings, the browser's language, `insert` and `changed`, and calls out through those two alone. Phases `idle`, `listening` (waiting for the microphone until the browser starts) and `finishing`. Final phrases go into the box as they arrive; the words still being heard are kept apart. The microphone again, Enter, Send, the time limit and the page being hidden finish (stop, then wait up to `voice.finish_wait` for the last words); Escape stops at once and keeps the words still being heard; typing in the box stops at once and leaves them out; the browser ending or an error ends it with its own sentence. `holdsSend`, which the composer asks first, finishes instead of sending while dictation runs (decision 0502). Also `insertAt` (words at a point, removing nothing), `readVoiceSettings` and `durationMs` (Go durations). The approach and the wording of the errors are New Keystone's `voice.js`, rewritten.
- **The screen**: `DictationControls.tsx` with `useDictation` (the browser's `SpeechRecognition` or `webkitSpeechRecognition`, `setTimeout`, the insertion point at the cursor, the caret after the words, the focus in the box, the settings read through `GET /api/v1/settings` each time a composer opens), `DictationButton` (just before the send button; `aria-pressed`, a label for each state, copper and pulsing while listening, dimmed but focusable with its reason when the browser cannot) and `DictationStatus` (a polite live region with a dot, the state or the reason, and the interim words in italics). `dictation.css` from the tokens, with no motion under reduced motion. A `mic` icon.
- **Settings**: a new `settings/voice.json`, lane `voice`: `voice.dictation` (true, tenant), `voice.language` (empty, the browser's own, tenant), `voice.interim` (true, tenant), `voice.max_listen` (2m, tenant; 0s no limit), `voice.finish_wait` (5s, install).
- **The header**: `Permissions-Policy: microphone=(self)` on every answer, and a test that it and `default-src 'self'` are on the page, a route, a file and an API path. Where the audio goes is decision 0500.
- **The proof**: `web/app/test/dictation.test.ts`, 15 tests run by `node --test` with no package (`npm test`, decision 0501); a new standing check, `dictation`, in `tools/verify/dictation.go`. `verify` is 36 of 36.
- The write-up: `docs/architecture/dictation.md` with its `componentDocs` line (an `init` in `tools/verify/dictation.go`) and overview row, decisions 0500 to 0502, and a paragraph each in `web.md` and `tools.md`.

## What was learned

- **Windows does not tell `Dictation.tsx` from `dictation.ts`**: `import "./Dictation"` would have resolved to the machine, since Vite tries `.ts` before `.tsx`. The React part is `DictationControls.tsx`.
- **Node 24 runs TypeScript as it is** (`node --test file.ts`), which gives the web app a test runner with no package, as long as the file under test imports nothing that is not TypeScript Node can strip, and the test imports it with its `.ts` ending, outside `src` so `tsc` does not see it. `node --test` with a folder does not work; it takes files or globs.
- **The browser pane has `webkitSpeechRecognition`**, so the real recognizer would have been used; a stand-in put on `window.SpeechRecognition` before the composer opened drove the screen without a microphone.
- Tests that passed at once were broken on purpose: with `holdsSend` answering false, the send test failed.
