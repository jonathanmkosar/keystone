## What was learned

- Much of the store side was there already: the built-in store kept and searched vectors since P6-02, and the Waymarks adapter pushed them under the embedder token since P6-03. What was missing was the model, the refresh, the query vector and the rule. The one gap in the stores was that the built-in store could not tell a vector of the old model from one of the new; naming the model in `VectorsNeeded` closed it, and matches what Waymarks does through its settings.
- Waymarks' hybrid search scores a hit found by vector by its place (`1/(61+i)`), never by its nearness, so Keystone cannot drop a far page: with Waymarks, the nearest pages join every message. The built-in store gives a cosine, and a threshold of 0.3 keeps a message about nothing empty. This is an owner question.
- The fake's first vectors weighed every word alike, and a question shared "the" and "is" with every page. Recall embeds the same query it searches words with, without the ignored words, and the fake weighs a meaning four times another word.
- A refresh that lists the pages needing a vector and skips the sensitive ones could starve behind them with a hosted model, so the store is asked for more pages (`knowledge.embedding_list_max`) than one refresh sends.
- The runner reads its settings registry on first use. The routes called the settings before that and read nothing; both entry points now set the runner up first.
- The verify fake Waymarks had no vectors; three routes in Waymarks' shape, from its own source, let the check run on both stores.
- The migration was first stamped 214753; reboot moved on with 224500 (unattended spend), so it is 234753, later than every one on reboot at d564a08.
- The check passed first time, which proves nothing about it. Three faults were put in by hand and each was caught: `MayEmbed` and `MayEmbedQuery` always yes (the hosted model got the sensitive page and the held chat's message), and no query vector (by words and meaning answered 3 of 6, the same as words alone). They were taken out again.

## Questions for the owner

1. Which embedding model should the home gateway serve, for Keystone and for Waymarks?

   Recommended: a small open embedding model the gateway serves over the OpenAI-style `/v1/embeddings`, such as nomic-embed-text (768 numbers) or bge-m3 (1024 numbers), registered as a local entry with the `embedding` capability. Waymarks is then set to the same name (`search.embedding_model`, the entry's `provider_model`) and size (`search.vector_size`).

   Assumed meanwhile: no embedding model; recall is by words alone, as before.

   Provisional because of it: P6-08's live check.

2. The embedder token (knowledge 003, decision 0090): this item uses it as built, for listing the pages that need a vector and pushing them.

   Recommended: as before, a third token with read and page-write rights.

   Assumed meanwhile: the adapter takes an optional embedder token; without it, Waymarks is searched by words alone.

   Provisional because of it: P6-08's live check, with question 1.

3. Waymarks' hybrid search gives a hit found by vector its place, not how near it is, so Keystone cannot leave a far page out: with Waymarks, the nearest pages join every message, up to `knowledge.recall_meaning_max_results`.

   Recommended: give Waymarks' hybrid hits the cosine as a field of their own (or a `min_cosine` in the request), and set `knowledge.recall_meaning_min_score_waymarks` on it once it is there.

   Assumed meanwhile: the threshold ships at 0, and five pages at most join.

   Provisional because of it: nothing.

4. Should the pause switch for unattended work (`agents.paused`, P8-05) hold the background embedding refresh too?

   Recommended: yes, for an embedding model that is not local; a local one costs nothing and goes on.

   Assumed meanwhile: the refresh has its own reserve ($0.50 a month, inside the cap) and its own switch, `knowledge.embeddings`, and the pause switch does not hold it.

   Provisional because of it: nothing.

5. The built-in store's threshold for a page found by meaning is a cosine of 0.3, a guess.

   Recommended: tune it on the real model's scores once one is in use.

   Assumed meanwhile: 0.3.

   Provisional because of it: nothing.
