## What was learned

- The Anthropic adapter already had two forms of thinking chosen by model name. Routing effort through that path, rather than writing a budget for "other models" as the spec's table reads, keeps an adaptive-only model from being sent a budget it would refuse.
- On some current Anthropic models, a request with no `thinking` field does not think at all, so effort alone would ask for depth that never happens. A model on the effort list with `thinking` gets adaptive thinking beside its effort.
- A new field in an aligned struct makes gofmt rewrite every line of the block, which would have shown as a change to lines that did not change and made the merge with P14-02 harder. Each new wire field sits after a blank line instead.
- The Chat Completions fixtures are one exchange to a file, so the effort exchanges live in a folder of their own with a list, as the embedding exchanges do, and have their own small test.
