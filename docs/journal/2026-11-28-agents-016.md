## What was done

P14-04 already routes every turn of a chat on Auto that names no model, so nothing new routes anything: the verdict row, the route, the route event and the effort follow.

Work on Auto keeps its `model` empty, so its turns name none. Off Auto, each names the model it did before. `internal/jobs/auto.go` and `internal/subagents/auto.go` hold the small `onAuto` helpers.
- No migration, no setting, no route, no field of the API.

## What was learned

- **P14-04 had left the door open.** Decision 0690 made the chats Keystone makes itself start off Auto "until P14-06 decides", with `turn.SetChatAuto` there for it. Putting the chat on Auto, and sending turns that name no model, was the whole of the routing.
- **Before this item, unattended work named a model on every turn**: the parent chat's model, through `ChatModel`. A turn that names a model takes its chat off Auto (decision 0690), so a work chat could never have been on Auto, even by hand. Work on Auto now keeps `model` empty.
- **A scheduled run's task has a request id of its own**, not the run's `schedule_runs.request_id`, which is the call that started it. Its verdict and decision call are under the task's.
- **A carry-on round is routed afresh**, so a task's hard first round on the hosted step can come back down to the local step for the round after its step limit: the test proves it.
- **The router knows nothing of the cap.** At the cap Auto may choose a hosted step, which the gate refuses and the person's chain replaces; a ladder's local step is not a fallback unless it is in the chain. Raised below.
- **`verify` is load-sensitive while seven lanes build at once.** The load test's timing budgets, its turns, and the real-browser test each failed one run and passed the next; none touches unattended work.

## Questions for the owner

- **At the monthly cap or a run's budget, should Auto skip its hosted steps, so unattended work moves down the ladder to a local step?** As built, no (decision 0733): the router may choose a hosted step, the gate refuses it, and the person's fallback chain decides, as decision 0392 has it; a ladder whose local step is not in the chain stops the run. Recommended: yes, skip hosted steps at the cap as a down local machine's steps are skipped, with the reason line saying so, in P14-05 or a small item after it. Assumed meanwhile: the chain decides.
- **Should unattended work follow `router.auto_default`, the admin's switch for new chats?** As built, yes (decision 0730): with the switch off, unattended work names the model it did before Auto. Recommended: keep it; a separate switch only if the owner wants people's chats on picked models and unattended work on Auto. Assumed meanwhile: one switch.
