## What was learned

- The spec's rules are four since the owner's change of November 27: the fifth, the chat's floor, went when every message became routed afresh. The item's "five rules" is from before that.
- Asking `routing.Plan` for each step alone gives the router rules 1 and 2 and the skip of an unknown name for nothing, and makes "never widens the plan" a property a test can hold every candidate to.
- `internal/budget` reads settings through `FromSettings`, so a guard cannot import it, even for one function. The window check is four lines mirrored, with a test that compares them with `budget.Measure` edge by edge.
- A timeout and an unreachable endpoint look alike to a person and differently to the seam. The brief's own risk, a verdict waiting behind the chat model on one machine, decided that only `unavailable` means the machine is down (decision 0671).
- An answer the decision model gives for a step that is not a candidate is not usable, so a held chat's verdict cannot name a hosted step at all: it is not among its options, and an answer outside them is the rules alone.
