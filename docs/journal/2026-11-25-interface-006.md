## What was done

An artifact belongs to its chat, with a type (code with a language, HTML, React, SVG, Mermaid, Markdown), a title and numbered versions: rows in `artifacts` and `artifact_versions` (migration `20261125T221947_artifacts.sql`), each version's content a file in the tenant's `artifacts/` folder, `<chat>/<artifact>/v<n>`, through `Store.Artifacts()`. Every version is kept; an update or a restore writes a new one. Deleting the chat removes the folder (one call, with its check, in `chat.Delete`). A version written in a held chat is marked sensitive, and every artifact of a held chat reads as sensitive.

## What was learned

- **The desktop app's browser pane refuses every request from a page of no origin** (`ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT`): a frame with a `sandbox` attribute, and a script a sandboxed page asks for. The first build loaded the renderers from `/sandbox/` with `<script src>`; writing them into the page removed the request, and with it every question of what `'self'`, the session cookie and the Host mean in a page of no origin. The frame inside the panel still cannot be seen in that pane.
- **Mermaid needs no `'unsafe-eval'`**: at its strict level it drew under `script-src 'unsafe-inline'` alone.
- **A page drawn outside the app's routes has no route parameters**: the publish page read an empty id until it matched its own address.
- **Writing a 5 MB script into a page is safe only once `</script`, `<script` and `<!--` are gone from it**: Mermaid's bundle holds `<!--` three times, in DOMPurify's and marked's strings and a regular expression. `\x3C` reads as `<` in each of those places.
- **A recorded turn cannot know an id it has not seen**: `update_artifact` takes an artifact's exact title as well, which also helps a model whose turn with the id was summarised away.
- Tests that passed at once were broken on purpose: the sandbox directive taken out of the policy, `allow-same-origin` let through `SandboxTokens`, and the hold check taken out of `Create` each failed their tests.
