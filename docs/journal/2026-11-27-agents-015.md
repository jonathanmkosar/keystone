## What was learned

- **A refused or cooling provider hides the next answer.** The `web search` surface of `no keys in output` asks one provider five things in a row; with states, its refusal now leaves that provider refused and the later answers are not sent to it. The new surface asks each answer of a fresh provider, for both vaults, so the check as a whole covers at least what it did.
- **A spent quota's "nothing left" outlived the provider's recovery.** A soft-test search that succeeded put the provider straight back out, because the 0 recorded when it went out, less the searches since, was still at the reserve. An out provider that answers now forgets that report (decision 0685).
- **A search's audit entries are counted by `verify`**, one before and one after, so the record of which provider answered could not be a new entry; it is the result's last line (decision 0684).
- **Windows will not remove a folder with a database open in it**, so the install's `search.db` is never held open: each piece of work opens and closes it.
- The tool registry's audit entry keeps the result as JSON, so `web.Answered` reads the result text, not the entry.

The item's proof, each on stand-ins shaped as Tavily on `httptest`, with a clock of the test's own:

- A provider out of quota is passed over and the next answers; the next search goes straight to the next. The order follows `order`, `free_first` (free quota used first, then the paid one, with its cost counted) and `weighted` (by the share of quota left, unknown as half, the provider with no quota last; and the draw's shares over a thousand draws). A rate limit cools a provider for the 120 seconds it gave, for `web.cooling_for` with none, and an hour's wait is out for the hour. Three failures put one in `failing`. A refused key is never searched or checked again through five sweeps two days apart, until it is changed.
- Soft tests: back at a known reset, December 1, and not a second before; back after a free check with quota left (none left doubles the wait first); one real search let through first, alone, with waits of 30m, 1h, 2h and 2h at a cap of 2h, and back when it answers; never two tests at once, by search or by sweep.
- Brave's headers and Tavily's usage check are read into what is left, less the searches since; Keystone's own count against the monthly quota and the reserve.
- A person's own provider is tried before the install's, even a free install one under `free_first`; the install's answers with the install's key when the person's is out. No key in a result, a view, the audit log, the person's folder or `data/admin/`; the `search providers` surface covers both vaults in `verify`.
- Every search is in the audit log with the provider that answered and those passed over, and every provider's line when none answered.
- Web tools stay off in a held chat, with an own and an install provider.
- The move: a person with `provider.tavily` and no entries searches and is answered through Tavily with their key, the entry is first with `provider.tavily` as its key, it is made once, and the sweep moves a second person before any search.
