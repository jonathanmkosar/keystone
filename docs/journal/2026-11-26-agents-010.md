## What was done

- **`internal/subagents`**, a new package. The tool `run_subagents` (`agents`: a task each, and a title, context, model and budget) checks the call, writes each sub-agent's row and its work chat ("Sub-agent: …") and registers it with `unattended.Register` as `KindSubagent` in the same transaction, with the person's `agents.subagent_budget_usd` or a smaller one the model asked for (decision 0482). A supervisor, under the turn runner, sends each sub-agent one turn of its chat under its own request id, `agents.subagents_at_once` at a time, reads the switch that pauses every agent at every look, follows the turns, tells each chat of its notices, and hands the tool a section per sub-agent: status, steps, spend, notice, its result cut at `agents.subagent_result_max_chars`, and its chat's id for the whole transcript. A sub-agent's chat refuses `run_subagents`; a turn starts at most `agents.subagents_per_turn`. Stop from a lane, the parent's stop, `agents.subagent_timeout`, the pause switch and a restart each end the sub-agents with a status that says who (decisions 0480, 0484, 0485). Three routes: `GET /chats/{id}/subagents` (by `call` or `turn`), `GET …/{sub}`, `POST …/{sub}/stop`.
- **`internal/subagents/carry`**, the guard of fixed rules 2 and 3: `Down` carries the parent's outside and held marks into a sub-agent's chat; `Up` passes a held sub-agent's result only to a held parent, and marks the parent and the result when a sub-agent read outside content (decision 0483). It imports nothing of Keystone's.
- **Shared code, few lines**: `turn.Options.MaxSteps` and `internal/turn/subagents.go`, and one changed line in `internal/turn/tools.go`; `tools.Tool.Timeout` and `tools.Input.CallID` in `internal/tools/tools.go`; added lines in `internal/app` and the new `internal/app/subagents.go`.
- **The lanes**: `web/app/src/Subagents.tsx`, `subagentCalls.ts` and `subagents.css`; one line in `Conversation.tsx` and an entry in `steps.ts`. The `run_subagents` step is the approved row "Ran 3 sub-agents in parallel", with a lane for each sub-agent under it: state, where it is, spend against budget, its steps (from its chat), its result, its approval card answered in place, Open its chat, and Stop. `web/dist/` is not committed.

## What was learned

- The jobs pattern carried over almost whole: a sub-agent built on turns of its own chat got recording, approvals, holds, budgets, the cap's fallback and stop for nothing. What turns did not have was a step limit per chat, and a tool that may run for longer than `agents.tool_timeout`.
- The tool's own goroutine is not waited for when a turn stops or the runner closes, and the store it is given closes with the turn's queue. The supervisor therefore runs under `turn.Runner.Go` with a store of its own, and the tool only waits on it.
- A registry tool cannot know the parent turn's route, so it cannot tell whether every model the parent may reach could read a held sub-agent's result. Holding the parent mid-turn would not change the route already chosen, so the result is kept back instead.
- A turn on a real adapter is followed by a title call to the same model, so a test that reads "the parent's last call" reads the title request; the tests read the newest call that carries a tool result.
- Rewriting a Go file through PowerShell 5.1's `Get-Content`/`Set-Content` turned its curly quotes into mojibake and added a byte-order mark; the file was repaired, and edits went through the editor after that.
