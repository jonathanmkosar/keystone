## What was done

- **`internal/wikijobs`**, a new package. The five jobs: Waymarks' agent-kit `compile`, `lint`, `upkeep` and `fix_issues`, with the kit's grants word for word, a principal name and a vault secret each (`knowledge.waymarks.<job>`), and the read tools of Waymarks' MCP server by the kit's names; and `builtin_compile` for the built-in store. Each job is a schedule it owns (`wiki_jobs`), made the first time it is switched on and kept in step with its settings by a digest; off, or unable to run, pauses it. A run is the schedule's background task in a chat of its own. `Connectors` offers the job's tools in its runs' chats only, under its own token, and holds the chat for sensitive content at the first call; `Gate` refuses a hosted model for any call of a wiki run; `SkipMemory` keeps the memory pass out. When a run's task is done, the runner reads its JSON answer, checks it whole with `scope.Check`, writes it as Waymarks batches under the job's token (with `[[new:ref]]` links resolved to the ids Waymarks gives, and a page written again in the batch when it links to a later one) or as built-in pages, records each write in the audit log, and writes a note in the run's chat (`wiki_runs`). Withdrawn sources are left out of every tool and refused as citations. Four routes: `GET /wiki-jobs`, `POST /wiki-jobs/{job}/on`, `…/off`, `…/run`.
- **`internal/wikijobs/scope`**, the guard of fixed rules 2 and 3: `MayServe` (local or trusted only), `Check` (the grant's actions, citations the job may make, a sensitive source only in a sensitive space, the limits), `Resolve`. It imports nothing of Keystone's.
- **Shared code, few lines**: `turn.Options.SkipMemory` and one check in `memoryLater`; `schedules.ErrNotFound`, `ErrInvalid` and `ErrRunOpen` in a new file; `builtin.Uncited` and `builtin.SpaceLabels` in a new file; the wiring in `internal/app` (added lines and `wikijobs.go`).
- **The screen**: `WikiJobs.tsx` and `wiki-jobs.css`, a Wiki upkeep section of the Scheduled screen. `web/dist/` is not committed: the integrator rebuilds it.

## What was learned

- The approval rule decides the shape of the jobs. A source is outside content, so a write tool would ask the person on every page of every run. Writing from the job's answer, through its grant, as the memory pass writes from its model's answer, keeps the jobs unattended without touching `internal/tools/approval` (decision 0441). The owner is asked whether that is what he wants.
- Holding the run's chat makes every later call of it, recaps and compaction included, go only to local or trusted models with no new routing code; the gate covers the first call, whose route is planned before the hold. A refusal of the kind `unavailable` falls back without being taken for the cap (decision 0442).
- A scheduled run's turns are sent under its task's own request id, not the run's: the writes are recorded under the task's, so a run's whole story is one request id.
- A JSON reply decoded into a struct that was filled before keeps the old value of a field the reply omits. The test that read a job's state twice into one variable saw a stale `problem`; the fix was in the test.
- The real Waymarks names each call `api.<route>` in its audit log, and its health route answers whether or not a token is sent. Its batch write takes a new page again at the same path to replace it, which the two-pass link writing uses.
- The built-in adapter's name is `built-in`, not `builtin`.

## Questions for the owner

- **Should a wiki job's writes, after it read sources, run under its grant without your answer to each?** Recommended: yes, as built: the kit's grant, the batch you can revert in Waymarks, and Waymarks' suspension of an agent whose batches go wrong are the guards the agent kit intends for unattended agents. The other way is for each batch to ask in the run's chat, which makes the jobs wait for you. Assumed meanwhile: as recommended.
- **Which jobs to switch on.** Recommended: the upkeep job alone, daily at 04:00, as the kit advises, on your local Qwen (`routing.memory_model`); add compile at 02:30 if pages should follow chats sooner. Lint and fix issues are in upkeep's round. Assumed meanwhile: all off.
- **Decision 0371's notice source for withdrawn sources.** Keystone's own jobs now skip withdrawn sources themselves (decision 0443). Recommended: send no notice source; it would only matter for agents outside Keystone, and after the cutover (P12-01) there are none. Assumed meanwhile: none is sent.
